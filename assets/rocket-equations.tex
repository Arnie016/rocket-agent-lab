{\Large Rocket Agent Lab: classroom flight model}

\vspace{0.35in}

\[
F_{\rm net}=T-W-D=T-mg-\frac{1}{2}\rho v^2 C_D A
\]

\[
a=\frac{F_{\rm net}}{m}
\]

\[
m_0=m_{\rm dry}+m_{\rm propellant}+m_{\rm payload}
\qquad
m_f=m_{\rm dry}+m_{\rm payload}
\]

\[
\Delta v=I_{sp}g_0\ln\left(\frac{m_0}{m_f}\right)
\]

\[
\Delta v_{\rm staged}\approx G_{\rm stage}I_{sp}g_0
\ln\left(\frac{m_0}{m_f}\right)
\]

\[
\frac{T}{W}=\frac{T}{m_0g_0}>1
\]

\[
q=\frac{1}{2}\rho v^2
\qquad
\rho(h)=\rho_0e^{-h/H}
\]

\[
{\rm payload\ margin}=m_{\rm payload,max}-m_{\rm payload}
\]

\[
{\rm mission\ margin}=\Delta v_{\rm staged}-\Delta v_{\rm required}
\]

\vspace{0.25in}

The web app uses these equations as a transparent teaching model. It is not a
full launch simulator: it is a hands-on layer for seeing how payload, fuel
load, drag area, thrust-to-weight ratio, dynamic pressure, and mass ratio move
together.
